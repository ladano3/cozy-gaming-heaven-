Games to Play in Bed

Lantern Light Under the Covers

Low-glare, one-handed, and soft-paced experiences crafted for winding down before sleep.

Designing for the Midnight Hour

The ideal bedtime game asks very little of your reflexes and nothing of your adrenaline. Warm, muted color palettes protect tired eyes, while turn-based or mouse-only controls let you stay tucked comfortably beneath a blanket.

Audio That Lulls Rather Than Startles

Gentle acoustic guitars, distant crickets, and soft page-turn sound effects help transition the mind from daytime alertness to restful stillness—never jarring you awake with sudden alarms or boss music.

Curated Titles to Begin With

Kind Words (PC) lets you write and receive anonymous, encouraging letters to real people set to lo-fi chillhop. Cozy Grove (Switch, PC, Mobile) offers 30 minutes of daily island tasks designed explicitly not to be binged.
