Cozy Games on PC

Desk Lamps & Expansive Worlds

Deep creative sandboxes, rich modding communities, and ambient desktop companions.

The Creative Freedom of Mouse & Keyboard

PC gaming elevates cozy builders and management sims with effortless drag-and-drop precision, ultra-wide scenic vistas, and thriving Steam Workshop communities that keep beloved games fresh for years.

Idle Companions While You Work

A uniquely PC phenomenon is the 'bottom-of-screen' cozy game—tiny terrariums, study rooms, or duck ponds that sit peacefully alongside your browser while you study or read.

Curated Titles to Begin With

Tiny Glade (PC) is a pure, gridless diorama builder where castles, sheep, and ivy sprout organically with every click. Rusty's Retirement (PC) sits neatly at the bottom of your monitor while robots tend crops.
